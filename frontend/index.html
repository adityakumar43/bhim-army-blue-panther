Bhim Army Blue Panther (भीम आर्मी ब्लू पैंथर)

A social organisation in India working to uplift Dalit, backward, minority, women and tribal (Adivasi) communities and to raise a strong voice against crimes, exploitation and atrocities committed against them. Educate, Organise, Struggle (शिक्षित बनो, संगठित रहो, संघर्ष करो).
